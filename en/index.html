What you train

It explains the why.

You log the session and the engine reads your load and your freshness: it raises or lowers next week’s volume and brings the deload forward if needed. And it tells you which of your own data decided it.

What it learns about you

It improves with you.

Two people on the same plan do not progress the same. It learns the rate you respond at, in strength and in cardio, and paces the progression to that, not to the average.

What you tell it

Smart substitutions.

“I hate squats”, “shoulder is off”, “I’m training at home this week”. It is not a one-day patch: it stays on your profile, and next week the plan already comes with it built in.
