Reported pain: right shoulder on the overhead press.
Overhead press and dips stress the cuff → I remove them today.
I keep the push stimulus with shoulder-safe angles.
I add rotator cuff activation before starting to prevent injury.
Done. I'm dropping the overhead press and the dips so we don't strain the shoulder, and we'll start with a rotator cuff warm-up. Today's session 👇
