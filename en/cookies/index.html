Cookie policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This is a courtesy translation. If the two versions differ, the Spanish one prevails.

This page explains what 2trainapp.com stores in your browser. We don’t use analytics, advertising or third-party cookies, and we don’t load any external measurement tools.

1. What we store

We only ask your permission for one thing: remembering which campaign or website brought you here (the UTM parameters in the URL, Google or Meta click IDs, the landing page and the referring website), so we know which channels bring us users. If you accept, we store it in your browser for 90 days and it travels with your beta sign-up if you sign up. If you reject it, nothing is stored and that origin only applies to the current visit.

2. Details

  • cookie_consent · first-party cookie, essential · remembers whether you accept or reject · 1 year.
  • 2t_attr · first-party local storage, only if you accept · remembers which campaign brought you here, to attach it to your sign-up · 90 days.

3. Changing your choice

You can change it at any time from “Cookie preferences” in the site footer. If you reject, we delete anything already stored. You can also clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings.

4. If we add analytics or marketing

We’ll update this page with the details of each cookie and ask for your permission before setting them.
