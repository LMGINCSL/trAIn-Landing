This policy explains what data 2trAIn processes, why it uses it and your rights under the GDPR and Spanish data protection law.

1. Data controller

Controller: LMGINC SL (tax ID B93905438), registered at Calle Lagasca 95, 28006 Madrid (Spain), the company operating the 2trAIn service. Privacy contact: contact@2trainapp.com. No Data Protection Officer has been appointed at this stage; inquiries and requests are handled at that address.

2. Data we process

Depending on the features you use, we process:

  • Account: email, name, sign-in provider and identifiers.
  • Profile: age, sex, measurements, level, goals, availability and equipment.
  • Health: injuries, discomfort, limitations, fatigue and information you choose to share. These are special-category data.
  • Training: plans, exercises, sets, reps, weight, effort, rests, feedback and progress.
  • Coach conversations and product feedback.
  • If you connect them in the app, the Apple Health or Health Connect data you authorize (for example, steps, sleep, heart rate, workouts and weight), read-only.
  • Location: the GPS route of outdoor cardio sessions, only while the session is running.
  • Minimum technical data for security and operation.
  • Beta sign-up on the website: your email, language, the referral code of the person who shared the link with you (if any) and how you reached the site: campaign parameters (UTM), a Google or Meta ad click identifier (gclid, fbclid) and the landing and sign-up pages. To prevent abuse we also keep the IP address of each sign-up attempt for a limited time.

If you accept cookies (details in the cookie policy), the origin of your visit is stored in your browser for up to 90 days so it isn't lost if you come back another day; if not, it only applies to that visit and nothing is stored on your device.

3. Purposes and legal basis

We use data to manage your account, create and adapt your plan, record progress, provide the chat feature and keep the service secure. The legal basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR). Health data is processed only with your explicit consent (Art. 9.2.a). Fraud prevention and minimum technical metrics rely on our legitimate interests (Art. 6.1.f). When you sign up for the beta we send you the access email (Art. 6.1.b), and we use the origin of your visit to learn which channels and campaigns bring us users, based on our legitimate interests (Art. 6.1.f); you can object by writing to us.

4. Health-data consent

You may withdraw consent at any time through privacy settings or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect earlier processing, but may prevent safe personalization based on your injuries or limitations.

5. AI and profiling

2trAIn uses AI to generate explanations and personalize plans. Recommendations have no legal effect and you may ignore them, express your view or request human review at contact@2trainapp.com.

6. Providers and transfers

We do not sell your data. To operate the service we use hosting and database (Google Cloud Platform, EU region in Belgium), transactional email (Resend), maps (MapTiler, only to draw the map behind your routes), AI (Anthropic), website (Vercel) and beta sign-up (Supabase) processors, plus Google or Apple if you choose social sign-in. Chat may send Anthropic your message and limited training context, without your name, email or internal user ID. Some of these providers (Anthropic, Resend, Vercel or Supabase) may process data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the US; in those cases the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

7. Retention

We retain data while your account is active and for the time needed to handle deletion, legal obligations and backups. When you close your account, linked data is deleted or anonymized within 30 days unless the law requires retention. Beta sign-up data from the website is kept while the beta lasts or until you ask us to delete it, and the IP address of sign-up attempts is deleted after 7 days.

8. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and your rights regarding automated decision-making, by emailing contact@2trainapp.com with the subject “GDPR Rights”. You may also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

9. Security

We use HTTPS, restricted access, protected credentials and technical controls against unauthorized access, loss or alteration. No system is infallible; if we detect a breach we will act as required by law.

10. Children

The service is intended for users aged 14 and over. If you are under 14, you must not create an account or sign up for the beta without consent from a parent or legal guardian.

11. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the service or the law changes. Material changes will be announced in advance. For any questions, email contact@2trainapp.com.
