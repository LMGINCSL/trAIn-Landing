The format

8 km of running. 8 stations in between.

Every kilometre of running ends at a station. This is the order of the standard format:

  1. 1SkiErgafter 1 km of running1,000 m
  2. 2Sled pushafter 1 km of running50 m
  3. 3Sled pullafter 1 km of running50 m
  4. 4Burpee broad jumpsafter 1 km of running80 m
  5. 5Rowingafter 1 km of running1,000 m
  6. 6Farmers carryafter 1 km of running200 m
  7. 7Sandbag lungesafter 1 km of running100 m
  8. 8Wall ballsafter 1 km of running100 reps

Weights change by division (Open, Pro, Doubles or Relay). Always check the official rulebook for your event.
